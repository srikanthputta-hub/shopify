# Policy drafts (paste into Shopify admin: Settings → Policies)

The connector lacks the `write_legal_policies` scope, so these could not be applied automatically.
They are generic drafts — have them reviewed (ideally by a lawyer) before going live.

## Shipping Policy
We ship across India. Orders are dispatched by our partner suppliers within 1–3 business days and typically delivered in 7–12 business days (shipped directly from our partner suppliers). Shipping charges, if any, are shown at checkout. A tracking link is emailed once your order ships. Delivery times may be affected by weather, festivals or courier delays.

## Refund & Return Policy
If you receive a damaged, defective or wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos. We will arrange a replacement or refund. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days; for COD orders, refunds are made by bank transfer or UPI.

## Privacy Policy
We collect the information you provide at checkout (name, address, phone, email) to process and deliver orders and to contact you about them. We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with payment, shipping and service providers needed to fulfil your order. You may contact us to access, correct or delete your data.

## Terms of Service
By using this store you agree to these terms. Prices are in INR and inclusive of applicable taxes. We may cancel orders affected by pricing or stock errors and will refund any payment made. Product images are illustrative. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
