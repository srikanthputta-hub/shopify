# Research notes (2026-10-01)

Sources: Shopify India blog, GoDaddy India, Unicommerce, bybtraction/growwwtech India setup guides.

## Demand signals for India
Top categories: apparel, phone accessories, skincare/beauty, health & wellness, home decor/kitchen.
Frequently cited winners: portable massage guns, smart water bottles, air fryers/kitchen gadgets,
decor and smart-home gadgets, pet products, 3-in-1 wireless chargers. Our 5 collections follow this.

## India setup essentials
- GST: Settings → Taxes and duties; enter GSTIN; rates 5/12/18/28% by category; prices tax-inclusive.
- Payments: Razorpay (KYC with PAN, GSTIN, bank proof); COD as a manual method; UPI.
- Shipping: Shiprocket / Delhivery apps; COD serviceability varies by pin code.
- Pages/policies: About, Contact, FAQ, Shipping, Refund, Privacy, Terms.
- Test with a real low-value order before launch.
